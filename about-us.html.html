About Us What Drives Us Our Team

This is what Bright Future Tutoring Centre can say about us.

This is a small educational business that provides the extra lessons and academic support students need in Mathematics, Physical Science, English and Life Science. This centre offers both in-person and online sessions, helping students improve their academic performance and prepare for exams.

We aim to create a supportive learning environment where learners can ask questions, develop their skills and build confidence. Our online and in-person sessions help learners prepare for assessments and work towards their academic goals.

What Drives Us

Our Vision

Our vision is to achieve a successful pass rate at the end of the school year and encourage more high school learners, especially those who are struggling academically, to participate in our programmes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to attract new learners and parents every year and encourage students to improve their academic marks. We want to reach learners nationwide in the next few years.

Our History

Bright Future Tutoring Centre has been operating since 2015 and continues to grow, with a reported pass rate of 98.99% over the years. We aim to expand internationally.
